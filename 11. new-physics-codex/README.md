## Core Thesis

Classical physics and modern quantum field theory remain trapped in the textures of the macro-interface. **The New Physics Codex** replaces the materialist vocabulary of particles, forces, and background spacetime with a rigorous, non-Von Neumann, quantum-informational stack ontology (**Project ADEYA**).

Within this framework:
* Physical laws are not immutable commandments, but high-level runtime abstraction protocols emergent from a singular, complex-valued Hilbert latent space.
* Fundamental interactions operate as infrastructure primitives: attention routing, local gauge re-encoding, hard thread encapsulation, and chiral type-refactoring.
* Universal constants ($c$, $\hbar$, $\alpha$) and cosmological horizons represent explicit hardware bandwidth bounds, mutex overheads, and memory-allocation limits.
