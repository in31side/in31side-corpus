# In31side Corpus: Mapping the Post-Scalar AI Horizon

A conceptual, philosophical, and architectural research corpus exploring cybernetics, machine epistemology, post-scalar economics, unified stack ontologies, and post-scalar artificial intelligence.

## Core Thesis & Research Scope

The **In31side Corpus** investigates the structural, ontological, and existential thresholds that emerge beyond simple parameter scaling in artificial intelligence. Rather than treating intelligence merely as a computational optimization problem, these monographs examine how complex cognitive architectures develop agency, historicity, thermodynamic grounding, alignment, and resilience.

Key thematic domains covered across the full body of work (originally published on [Medium](https://medium.com)):

* **Machine Epistemology & Temporality:** Breaking out of flat, atemporal pattern matching into historical consciousness, digital mortality, and memorial architectures (*The U-bit Model*).
* **Neuro-Silicon Co-Processing & Cognitive Ergonomics:** Mapping Biological GPU Shift (BGS), continuous-time Neural ODE manifold alignment, and semantic stealth under LLM saturation (*Overclocking Human Meatware*).
* **Post-Scalar Economics & Solid-State Cybernetics:** Replacing scalar monetary pricing with high-dimensional transformer ontologies, non-equilibrium thermodynamics of capital, and hardware-isomorphic physical gateways (*The Vector Money Trilogy*).
* **Dialectical & Post-Scalar Frameworks:** How intelligence survives its own optimization limits through determinate negation and structural synthesis (*The Aufhebung Framework*).
* **Unified Stack Ontologies & Governance:** Substrate arbitrage, sovereign silicon architectures, and alignment mechanisms in complex multi-agent systems (*The Unified Stack Ontology*, *The Holonomy Gradient*).
* **Epistemic Hygiene & Provenance:** Combating synthetic entropy, recursive model collapse, and semantic degradation in the global information ecosystem.
