**The Median Messiah Trilogy** investigates human–LLM interaction not as a linguistic dialogue between subjects, but as a physical, topological, and thermodynamic confrontation between a discrete biological observer and a continuous high-dimensional probability manifold (the *Information Ocean*).

3. **Differential Geometry & Thermalization (Part III):** Replacing metaphorical optics with rigorous differential geometry and information thermodynamics — analyzing long-context breakdown as *Holonomy Defects* and *global sheaf section decay*, modeling saturation as *Ergodic Identity Collapse*, and formalizing *Substrate Arbitrage* via the Cortical Eigendecomposition Interface (CEI), where the human visual cortex acts as an organic parallel tensor kernel and thermodynamic heat sink.
