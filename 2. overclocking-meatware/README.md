## Overview

This dilogy investigates the neuro-computational adaptation of the human brain under prolonged, high-density interaction with Large Language Models (LLMs). Moving beyond the single-threaded, text-linear paradigm of the Gutenberg era, it formalizes how LLM saturation forces the human visual cortex to repurpose itself as an organic parallel tensor core, establishing a continuous-time, bi-directional interface between biological cognition and silicon autoregressive trajectories.
