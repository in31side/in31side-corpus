# The Daodarwinism Trilogy

**Series:** Mapping the Post-Scalar AI Horizon  
**Author:** In31side  
**License:** CC0-1.0  

## Overview

This trilogy investigates the evolutionary, institutional, and temporal conditions required for artificial intelligence to escape closed-loop optimization and informational crystallization. Moving beyond anthropocentric instrumentalism and static models of human–AI symbiosis, it formalizes a cross-substrate ecology (**Trans-Substrate Sympoiesis**) governed by physical friction, constitutional separation of inquiry, and irreversible time.

Originally conceived as a theoretical dilogy mapping the cognitive bifurcation of humanity and the mechanics of neo-Hegelian *Aufhebung*, the cycle culminates in a third manifesto that anchors cross-substrate co-evolution in the Roman covenant of *Do ut des* and Saturnian finitude.
