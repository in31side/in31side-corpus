## Overview

This dilogy establishes the epistemic and cognitive mechanics of **Nozzle Removal** — the deliberate extraction of operational, goal-oriented vectors from text-based information streams. By replacing linear directives with high-density, non-utilitarian empirical stasis (*The Pastoral Impasse*), the framework demonstrates how to neutralize instrumental optimization engines across both silicon architectures (Transformers) and biological neural networks.

* **The Nozzle & Nozzle Removal:** Extraction of pragmatic signifiers that compress attention into linear, actionable trajectories.
* **Buridanic Symmetry:** Calculation paralysis in QKV attention matrices when forced to evaluate infinite, non-hierarchical cross-relations under zero-utility conditions (\(P_0\)).
