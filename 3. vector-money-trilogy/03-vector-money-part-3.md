Instead, systemic value becomes strictly proportional to the topological complexity of the information-physical gateways, manifesting as a multidimensional vector of shadow prices. Capital completes its geometric evolution, transforming from a social convention into a dissipative, non-equilibrium physical mechanism orchestrated at the speed of light.
