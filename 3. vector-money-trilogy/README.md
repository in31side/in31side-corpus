## Executive Abstract

**The Vector Money Trilogy** investigates the structural collapse of scalar monetary pricing in a post-AGI economy and establishes a rigorous mathematical, thermodynamic, and hardware-isomorphic replacement. 

Classical economics relies on a rank-1 projection—collapsing multi-dimensional physical, logistical, regulatory, and cognitive realities into a single one-dimensional price scalar. At post-AGI coordination speeds, this compression violates the Johnson-Lindenstrauss distortion bound, causing fatal informational erasure, structural mismatches, and speculative bubbles. Across three progressive installments, this monograph reconstructs capital not as an abstract accounting ledger, but as a **non-equilibrium thermodynamic vector field** orchestrated by a Large Multimodal Model (LMM) and grounded in physical matter through isolated hardware gateways.

## Core Theoretical Primitives

* **The Large Money Model (LMM):** Replaces static fiat balances with dense, multi-dimensional capability tensors ($\mathbf{v} \in \mathcal{M}$) spanning normalized thermodynamic, spatial-logistic, and informational axes ($\hat{J}, \hat{L}, \hat{I}$).
* **The Materialization Threshold ($m^{\ast}$):** The deterministic phase boundary where the exponential Landauer computational cost of simulating reality in silicon exceeds the sublinear kinetic energy cost of physical instantiation, forcing the vector to exit the virtual core into matter.
* **The Tensor Gate ($\partial\Omega$):** A solid-state cybernetic architecture mirroring the Field-Effect Transistor. By utilizing air-gapped Autonomous Mechanical Interfaces (AMI) operating via physical hardware interrupts on peripheral HIDs, the LMM modulates physical production channels without digital network coupling.
* **The Transistor Theory of Value:** In an economy experiencing simultaneous information deflation and automated material deflation, macroeconomic value migrates entirely to the topological complexity of information-physical interfaces, manifesting as a vector of Lagrange multipliers (shadow prices) governing phase-locked exergy throughput.
